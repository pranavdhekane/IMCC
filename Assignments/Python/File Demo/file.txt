The obsidian clocktower chimed thirteen times, its gears grinding against a breeze that tasted faintly of cinnamon and copper. 
Below, a stray neon umbrella drifted across the wet cobblestones, completely ignored by the mechanical swans patrolling the canal.
A librarian in silver spectacles paused on the bridge, carefully balancing a wooden crate of forgotten constellations.
She knew that if even one star escaped into the evening fog, the local train schedule would reset itself entirely by Tuesday morning.
